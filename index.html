About Us

Your Trusted Zanzibar Guide

Welcome to Acheda Zanzibar Tours – your gateway to the Spice Island. We are a locally-owned tour company dedicated to showing you the authentic soul of Zanzibar. With years of experience and deep roots in the community, we craft personalized adventures that blend culture, nature, and pure relaxation.

We believe that travel should be affordable and stress-free. That's why we offer competitive prices without compromising on quality. Our all-inclusive packages cover everything – from transport and meals to entrance fees and expert guides – so you can focus on making memories.
