About Acheda Zanzibar

Who We Are

Your Gateway to the Spice Island

Acheda Zanzibar Tours is a locally-owned tour company with deep roots in the community. We were born from a simple passion: to share the authentic beauty, culture, and warmth of Zanzibar with travelers from around the world.

With years of experience and a team of expert local guides, we craft personalized adventures that go beyond the typical tourist trail. From the bustling markets of Stone Town to the pristine sands of Paje Beach, we know the island inside out - and we love every corner of it.

  • Affordable & transparent – no hidden fees, just honest pricing
  • Reliable & punctual – we value your time and comfort
  • Locally rooted – our guides are born and raised in Zanzibar
  • Sustainable travel – we support local communities and protect our environment
